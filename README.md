# ApnoMart Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using Google Looker Studio (Data Studio) to analyze ApnoMart sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional performance.

## Project Details
- **Project:** The Insight Board – Business Intelligence Dashboard
- **Internship:** ApnoAI Data Analytics Internship
- **Prepared by:** Maha Lakshmi
- **Reporting Period:** May–October 2026
- **Tool Used:** Google Looker Studio (Data Studio)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Features
- Sales trend over time
- Sales comparison by product category
- Profit comparison by region
- Interactive region filter
- KPI cards for quick performance monitoring

## Key Business Insights
1. Furniture recorded the highest sales among the five product categories.
2. The Central region generated the highest profit, while the North region generated the lowest profit.
3. Sales peaked in July 2026. October represents a partial reporting period, so it should be compared carefully with complete months.

## Strategic Recommendation
Review product-category performance and regional profitability to identify opportunities for improving sales and profit. Monitor complete monthly reporting periods and evaluate targeted improvements based on business results.
